Measure lengths on a plane seen in perspective

  1. Load an image showing a rectangle in the plane you want to measure: a tile, window, sheet of paper, façade.
  2. Trace the rectangle with two pairs of lines.
  3. Calibrate and measure: mark a known length, then measure anything in that plane.

Runs locally. The image never leaves your computer.
